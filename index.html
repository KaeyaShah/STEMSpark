ABOUT STEMSPARK

Making STEM exciting, creative, and accessible.

STEMSpark is a community-focused STEM education initiative created to introduce elementary students to coding, engineering, robotics, science experiments, and collaborative problem-solving.

Instead of simply explaining STEM concepts, STEMSpark allows students to experience them. Students learn by building, questioning, testing ideas, making mistakes, improving their designs, and working together.

The program especially aims to make STEM opportunities more accessible while encouraging young students to see themselves as future scientists, engineers, programmers, and innovators.

WHAT STUDENTS EXPLORE

STEM is something you do.

Each STEMSpark activity encourages students to investigate a problem, create a solution, and learn through experience.

Creative Computing

ENGINEERING

ROBOTICS

SUSTAINABILITY

Built to continue after the first session.

STEMSpark was designed as more than a one-time STEM workshop. Activities, presentation decks, and instructional resources can be reused and adapted so future groups of students can continue benefiting from the program.

  • Reusable presentation and activity resources
  • Activities that can be repeated with future groups
  • Reusable or common materials whenever possible
  • Flexible lessons for different ages and group sizes
  • Activities designed to be taught by future volunteers
  • Feedback that can improve future STEMSpark sessions
